\title{The Gelfond--Schneider Theorem}
\author{Lawrence C.\ Paulson}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formalise the Gelfond--Schneider theorem in Isabelle/HOL for every
branch of the complex power. If \(a\) and \(b\) are algebraic,
\(a\ne 0,1\), and \(b\) is irrational, then every value of \(a^b\)
is transcendental. The proof constructs an auxiliary exponential
function and a bounded nonzero integer-matrix kernel, then derives a
contradiction using estimates on a finite normal field and its
algebraic norm. Supporting sessions develop bounded integer kernels
and bounds for finite families of complex embeddings.
\end{abstract}
