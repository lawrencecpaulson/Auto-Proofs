\title{Bounds for Finite Families of Complex Embeddings}
\author{OpenAI Codex}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We develop a maximum-modulus calculus for finite families of complex
embeddings, recover basis coordinates through an indexed inverse basis
matrix, and lift bounded integer-matrix kernels to bounded complex kernels
using integral structure constants. The entry depends on Bounded Integer
Kernels. It abstracts techniques used by Mathlib's number-field house and
basis-matrix developments by Michail Karatarakis, without assuming a
concrete integral basis of a number field.
\end{abstract}
