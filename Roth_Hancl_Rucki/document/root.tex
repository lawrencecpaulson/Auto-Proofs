\title{Han\v{c}l--Rucki Corollaries of Roth's Theorem}
\author{Lawrence C.\ Paulson}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The Han\v{c}l--Rucki transcendence results in the Archive of Formal
Proofs are stated in a locale that assumes Roth's theorem. This short
session interprets that locale using the formal proof of Roth's theorem
and exports its two results as unconditional corollaries.
\end{abstract}
