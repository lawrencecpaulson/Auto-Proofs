# AFP submission draft: Bivariate Resultants

**Title:** Bivariate Polynomial Specialisation and Resultants

**Author:** Lawrence C. Paulson

**Topic:** Mathematics/Algebra

**Proposed licence:** BSD

**Abstract:** We formalise factorisation and specialisation results for
bivariate complex polynomials. A derivative-resultant degree bound and a
multiplicity inequality control exceptional fibres, including fibres whose
degree drops after specialisation. We also develop the resultant of two
bivariate polynomials, with a weighted degree bound, a regular-fibre
common-root criterion, and a bound on common-root fibres. The
derivative-resultant arguments abstract Section 3 and Appendix A of Ben
Green's note *On Landau–Siegel zeros*.

**Source:** Ben Green, *On Landau–Siegel zeros*, unpublished note, 2026,
Section 3 and Appendix A.

`Diophantine_Universal_Pairs`, and `Schwartz_Zippel`, plus their ancestors.
