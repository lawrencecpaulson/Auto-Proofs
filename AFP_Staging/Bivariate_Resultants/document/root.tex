\title{Bivariate Polynomial Specialisation and Resultant Multiplicity}
\author{Lawrence C.\ Paulson}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formalise factorisation and specialisation results for bivariate complex
polynomials. A resultant degree bound and a multiplicity inequality control
exceptional fibres, including fibres whose degree drops after specialisation.
These results abstract the polynomial arguments in Section 3 and Appendix A of
Green's note on real Dirichlet characters and inert primes.
\end{abstract}
