\title{Bivariate Polynomial Specialisation and Resultants}
\author{Lawrence C.\ Paulson}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formalise factorisation and specialisation results for bivariate complex
polynomials. A derivative-resultant degree bound and a multiplicity inequality
control exceptional fibres, including fibres whose degree drops after
specialisation. We also develop the resultant of two bivariate polynomials,
with a weighted degree bound, a regular-fibre common-root criterion, and a
bound on common-root fibres. The derivative-resultant arguments abstract
Section 3 and Appendix A of Green's note \cite{green}.
\end{abstract}
