\title{Green's Theorem and Cauchy--Pompeiu for Planar Paths}
\author{Lawrence C.\ Paulson}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We prove Green's theorem for finite unions of interior-disjoint rectangles,
including boundary cancellation at T-junctions and a decomposition into
closed polygonal paths. We develop contour integration and winding numbers
for absolutely continuous paths, then derive winding-weighted Green identities
and an interior Cauchy--Pompeiu formula for a rectangular contour.
The development uses the local integration results of the Stokes entry.
\end{abstract}

\section{Relation to existing formalizations}
The AFP entry \emph{Green} by Mohammad Abdulaziz and Lawrence C.\ Paulson
formalizes a version of Green's theorem for domains described by boundary
graphs. This entry develops finite rectangular unions, explicit oriented
boundary walks, absolutely continuous paths, and Cauchy--Pompeiu identities.
