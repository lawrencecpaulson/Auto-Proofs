\title{Roth's Theorem on Diophantine Approximation}
\author{Lawrence C.\ Paulson}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formalise the classical real form of Roth's theorem in Isabelle/HOL.
For an irrational real algebraic number $\alpha$ and every $\delta>0$,
only finitely many reduced rational numbers $p/q$ with $q>0$ satisfy
$|\alpha-p/q|<q^{-2-\delta}$. The proof constructs a multivariate
auxiliary integer polynomial, transfers a large index from an algebraic
point to rational approximants, and derives the opposing index bound
from Roth's lemma. Supporting developments include Hasse derivatives,
polynomial height estimates, Wronskians, and a finite sequence selection
argument.
\end{abstract}
