# Polynomial Wronskians

This independent entry proves that binomial evaluation matrices at distinct
natural arguments are nonsingular and that the Hasse Wronskian of a linearly
independent finite family of rational polynomials is nonzero. It defines the
univariate Hasse derivative directly through iterated polynomial derivatives.
The Roth development imports these results and proves the link to its
multivariate Hasse derivative.
